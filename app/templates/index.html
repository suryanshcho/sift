Under the hood

What the AI actually does.

01

Python finds the rhythm

Not AI. A deterministic detector normalises merchant names, groups charges and checks the interval, amount stability and recency. It needs three monthly charges (or two annual) before it calls something recurring.

02

AI reads the summary

The live model receives merchant names, categories, prices, cadence, renewals, price changes and your usage decisions. It does not receive dates, transaction references or the raw CSV. It returns a ranked brief in strict JSON.

03

Code validates the answer

Every merchant the model names is matched back to a detected subscription. Savings are recalculated from the data, not trusted from the model. A model recommendation cannot override your “keep” decision.

04

You approve the action

Sift never connects to a bank, cancels a plan or sends an email. The final cancellation email is editable and copied by you. That is deliberate: the expensive decision stays human.
